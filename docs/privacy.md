# Privacy policy

_Last updated: 15 November 2026_

Budget is a personal budgeting app run by Abdul Miah for personal use. It is not offered to the public. This policy explains what data the app reads, where it is kept and who can see it.

## What the app reads

When you connect a bank account, you approve read-only access on your bank's own website. The app then reads, through Enable Banking:

- account names, types and currencies
- account balances
- transactions: date, amount, the name of the other party and the payment reference

The app cannot make payments, move money or change anything at your bank.

To sign in, the app also stores your email address and a hashed password.

## Where it is kept

- **Bank data and your sign-in account** are stored in a Supabase database hosted in the EU (Ireland). Only you can read your data. Every request must carry your sign-in, and the database refuses access to other users' rows.
- **On your phone**, the app keeps your sign-in session in the device's secure storage, and keeps a copy of your accounts and transactions to show them. Spending categories are worked out on the phone.

## Who it is shared with

Nobody, apart from the services needed to run the app:

- **Enable Banking Oy** (Finland), which connects to your bank under your consent and passes the data on. See [Enable Banking's privacy policy](https://enablebanking.com/privacy).
- **Supabase**, which hosts the database and the server code.

The data is never sold, used for advertising or analytics, or given to anyone else.

## How long it is kept

Bank access lasts at most 90 days, after which your bank asks you to approve it again. Data already downloaded is kept until you ask for it to be deleted.

## Your choices

- **Withdraw access** at any time, either in your bank's app or online banking (under connected or third-party apps) or by contacting Enable Banking. The app then stops receiving new data.
- **Ask for a copy, a correction or deletion** of your data by emailing abdulmuktadirmiah@gmail.com. Deletion covers your accounts, transactions and sign-in.
- **Complain** to the UK Information Commissioner's Office (ico.org.uk) if you think your data has been mishandled.
